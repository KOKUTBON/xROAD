# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Road Accidents Risk Modeling a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We commit to acting and interacting in ways that support an open, constructive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- showing empathy, patience, and respect for different viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive technical feedback;
- focusing discussion on evidence, public safety, and the needs of affected communities;
- acknowledging mistakes, correcting them, and learning from the experience;
- protecting sensitive road-safety data and respecting privacy, consent, and data rights;
- prioritizing the health of the project and community over individual advantage.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, intimidation, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks;
- sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and sustained disruption;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- using project data or community access to identify, target, or harm individuals;
- other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project in public spaces. Project spaces include issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, documentation, events, and project-related communication.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner through the contact method listed on the owner's GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue containing personal, sensitive, or safety-related details.

The maintainer will review reports promptly, protect the privacy and safety of reporters as far as reasonably possible, and take proportionate action. Responses may include a private correction, warning, temporary restriction, rejection of a contribution, or permanent removal from project spaces. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may be removed from project responsibilities by the project owner.
