# xROAD Signal — road safety design system

The frontend is designed from the visual language of the road itself. The system lives in three stylesheets imported after the structural `apps/web/app/globals.css`:

- `app/signal-system.css` — tokens, typography, shell, and every shared component.
- `app/signal-landing.css` — the public front door and sign-in.
- `app/workbench-utilities.css` — generated by `scripts/generate-workbench-utilities.mjs` for the workbenches authored with utility classes (forensics, incident coordination, connected fleets, TMC playbooks), remapped onto the Signal palette. Re-run the script after editing those components.

## Color

- **Asphalt** graphite (`--asphalt-*`) is the ground: the navigation rail, the public hero, sign plates, and the CTA stay asphalt in every theme.
- **Signal amber** (`--signal-amber`, #FFB400) is retroreflective sign yellow. Primary actions are amber plates with near-black ink; the current destination in the rail carries an amber lane marker. Text-safe amber (`--color-brand-primary`) is a deeper #8A4F00 on light surfaces.
- **Guide blue** marks information, **hazard red** marks danger and very-high-risk beacons, **go green** marks healthy/live state.
- The analytical Cividis risk ramp is unchanged, so maps and legends keep their meaning; it pairs naturally with the amber identity.

## Themes

Daylight (light), Night ops (dark), and High contrast. The appearance switch sits in the utility bar, the public nav, and sign-in; the choice persists and the no-flash init script applies it before first paint (falling back to the OS preference). Every text pair in all three themes is held to WCAG AA by `lib/contrast.test.ts`.

## Typography

Overpass — a descendant of Highway Gothic, the typeface family of road signage — served locally as a variable font, with Overpass Mono for labels, telemetry, and figures. Licenses are in `app/fonts/Overpass-OFL-LICENSE.txt`. Numerals are tabular in tables and metrics.

## Iconography and brand

- The mark is a warning-sign diamond in signal amber carrying a road in perspective, with a red hotspot at the vanishing point: *risk ahead*. SVG, favicon, Apple icon, manifest PNGs, and the social preview share it.
- `components/icons.tsx` is a road-semantic line set on a 24px grid (1.75 stroke) with a tonal "sign plate" fill: a folded map with a hotspot, a control-room gauge, a traffic cone for proposed works, a signal head for signal operations, converging trajectories for forensic reconstruction, a beacon for incidents, and so on. Icons inherit `currentColor` and are decorative; controls own the accessible name.

## Details

Mono uppercase eyebrows led by a small amber diamond; page headers underlined with a dashed lane marking; metric cards with a signal strip; hazard-striped warning states; kilometre-post steps on the public "how it works" route. The landing hero renders a foreshortened road in one-point perspective with animated lane markings and risk beacons — clearly labelled as a concept view, not observed data. All motion stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
